⚖️ **Owner rulings 2026-10-21, R1–R6, given by firing `docs/agent/prompts/STATION_ROWS_RELAY_BUILD_high.md` over the three hubless train reports (Erion, Xeryx, TheGodUncle) in `docs/agent/reports/STATION_ROWS_RELAY_20261021.md`; the decisions are its §12.6 as amended by §14.3, the rule and its weights §14.1.** Section references inside the rulings are that report's.

- **R1** On a line with no Train Hub, a row keeps its amount for its own station; what a
  station holds beyond that balances with the line as the base game balances, split by weight
  and water-filled (§14.1), so cargo for other lines passes through a station that joins lines.
  A station joins lines when it can exchange cargo on two or more routes. The reserve wins: a
  full Import or Balanced on a joining station leaves no room to pass cargo through.
- **R2** A line with an Elevator Depot takes this view whatever the Station rows toggle says, as
  a hub's network takes its rows; a depot half's row enters through the hub's existing read,
  for loading and for unloading.
- **R3** The depot-line dial pin of 2026-10-05, an agent's call, is dropped.
- **R4** A click to Import sets 100, or 50 on a station that joins lines and is not on a hub;
  a click to Export sets 0; a click to Default clears the row in the set being edited. The
  slider from Default makes Balanced at its value; from Export or Import it keeps the mode.
  Rows already saved keep their numbers.
- **R5** The depot's unset slider targets take the same defaults, Import 100 and Export 0, on
  every depot including hub-managed ones, in place of the dial percentage.
- **R6** Stock above an Export or Balanced number drains to untouched neighbours instead of
  stranding.

**What it does.** Each resource row on a vanilla train station's panel cycles Balanced / Export /
Import / Not accepted, with a slider for the target. Hubless stations keep their own settings
(`SMROptIn_station_rows`). A station on a hub's network uses the hub's settings
(`SMROptIn_distribution`, D17), and those rows are part of the hub.

**Code.** `Code/Opt_StationRows.lua` registers it. Its parts follow it, flat, because a
`ModItemCode`'s file is its name (FIX_POLICY §8):
- `StationRows_10_TrainFloor.lua`: the transient claims; its standing hold serves D17.
- `StationRows_40_TrainDistribution.lua`: the rows, train views and desired amounts.
- `StationRows_45_TrainDistributionUI.lua`: the native row hook.

**The gate.** `D.RowsOn(st)` is true on a hub's network always. Hubless, it is true while
`StationRows` or `TrainHub` is active. It is read per call by 40's hubless paths and by 45's
`network()`. Off, `on_deactivate` re-applies every station, which restores the vanilla desired
amounts and leaves the stored rows in place, unused.
