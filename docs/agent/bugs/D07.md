separately children with their schools/playgrounds) out of the production

## ⛔ Trigger E precedence half — ROUTED, not run, and why

The test needs the module to *want* to move a colonist while a player order
stops it. Three attempts failed to hold that fixture open:

1. `Opt_CohortHousing.lua:147-163` — the in-dome pass bails on
   `CheckForcedResidence()` (player order wins) **but also** on
   `res.exclusive_trait == trait` (already cohort-housed) and on
   `find_cohort_slot(dome, trait)` returning nil. With 0 in-dome-reachable
   slots at steady state there is **no motive**, so "it did not move them"
   would prove nothing.
2. Every slot created was consumed before a subject could be pinned — one
   candidate was moved into cohort housing while the pin was being set, which
   disqualified them (the pass never looks at cohort-housed colonists again).
3. `ForcedByUserLockTimeout = 3,600,000 ms = **5 sols**` (`__const.lua:154-156`)
   — the pin expires, so a long observation window can produce a legitimate
   move that reads as a precedence failure.

⇒ Routed as **TAKEABLE IN a co-run**, with the fixture built *before* any slot
exists: pin first, create the motive second.

## ⚖️ OWNER DESIGN DECISION OWED (routed to `PLAYTEST_CHECKLIST.md`)

Derived from Src this sitting, no fixture needed. The module's own header says
it is deliberate:

- the **in-dome** pass checks `CheckForcedResidence()` — a player-forced
  residence stops it;
- the **cross-dome** pass checks `CheckForcedDome()` — a player-forced
  *residence* does **not** stop it.

⇒ A player who pins a Senior to a specific residence can still have them
**emigrated to another dome entirely**, whenever their own dome has no free
cohort slot. That is the documented design. **Should pinning a colonist to a
residence also pin them to their dome?** Owner's call; nothing built either way.

## ⚖️ 2026-08-11 — OWNER RULING RECORDED: residence-pin does NOT pin to the dome

seems like a risk for a bunch of weird bug cases"* — is CONFIRMED as the D07
