### D15 — Service interest tags (design, display only)  `[tested-attended 2026-09-30 — Code/Opt_ServiceInterestTags.lua; owner sitting passed every leg and both §8 configurations]`

⚖️ **Owner ruling, 2026-09-28** (verbatim, relayed by the fix-pack seat's brief
`SERVICE_INTEREST_TAGS_BUILD_high.md`, consumed by this entry's commit):
*"can you actually author a brief in the opt in mod, to create a new interest tags opt in. That
would add this info in the construction panel when you hover of the building and add a ui panel
that has a interest / tags section for the build UI when its already out?"*

⚖️ **Owner ruling, 2026-09-29 — the placed-building surface becomes its own section**
(agreed in session, from screenshots of the Amusement Park, Art Store and Open Air Gym panels):
replace the Visitors row with an **"Interests" section** on every service building's panel. Its
popout carries the building's own description, its category and its visitor filter, plus the
trait bonuses (item 12 of `docs/FUTURE_IDEAS.md`, **un-parked by this ruling**). On short panels
(the Open Air Gym), losing the top description block when the new section tips the count is
**accepted**: the owner judged it *"just filler for the most part … repeatin the discription from
the construction menu"*, and its unique lines move into the popout. The build-menu hover keeps
its one Interests line, since a tooltip cannot have a popout. Build method left to the agent
("You can do it how you think is best").

⚖️ **Owner ruling, 2026-09-29 (later) — DLC and law lines are conditional:** the popout shows
the norman DLC's Foodie and Coffee Enthusiast lines only when that DLC's data is present, and the
Food Tours law's Tourist line **only while the law is active** (option 1 of two offered: the
alternative, always showing it marked "with the Food Tours law", was declined). The mystery-only
Infected cure stays out (*"skip the mystery part"*).

It asks for two surfaces: the build-menu hover of a service building, and an interests section on
a placed building's infopanel. Off by default, one Mod Options toggle (`ServiceInterestTags`),
vanilla UI with it off.

**Classification (FIX_POLICY §4).** A behaviour choice, display only: no rule about how colonists
choose or use services changes. Off is byte-vanilla behaviour. Not a donor-declined repair:
1.1.0 removed the display along with its strings, and nothing marks that as unintended.

**Why a player needs it — SOURCE, 1.1.1.405907.** Interests still drive play. `Service:SetCustomLabels`
files a building under one label per interest (`Buildings/Service.lua:170-230`), which
`Dome:GetService` (`Buildings/Dome.lua:3622`) searches for a colonist's daily interest. The Gamer
and Extrovert traits pay Sanity on entering a working building whose interests include Gaming or
Social (`Units/Colonist.lua:2671-2678`). The UI shows the category ("Stores") and never the
interests, so a player cannot see that an Electronics Store counts for Gamers.
**Prior art — SOURCE, 1.0.7.396349:** `Service:GetIPDescription` appended
`T{376972917568, "Services: <em><list></em>"}` (`Buildings/Service.lua:58-62`, again `:190-194`),
and `sectionVisitors` ended with `"Needs serviced: <ServiceList>"` (`XDef/sectionVisitors.generated.lua:44`).
Neither id is in the 1.1.1 `Lua` or `Data` trees (grep, 2026-09-28).

**Reachability — R1 live.** Hovering any service building in the build menu reaches
`X/BuildMenu.lua:832`; selecting a placed one builds `ipBuilding`, which creates `sectionVisitors`
(`XDef/ipBuilding.generated.lua:72`) and `sectionFoodService` (`:75`). No console step is involved.

## Trait effects in the popout

Research 2026-09-29 (a read-only agent over 1.1.1.405907; each row below re-checked or spot-checked
against its line). A building's own Health/Sanity/Comfort/Morale values are paid **at rest, per
dome and service category**, not per visit (`Lua/Stats.lua:219-241`); Medical pays per visit
(`MedicalCenter.lua:40-43`). The per-colonist differences a service makes are these traits:

| trait (display) | where | popout line | number source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gamer | interest Gaming | Sanity +param per visit | `TraitPresets.Gamer.param` = 10; `Colonist.lua:2671-2674` |
| Extrovert ("Party Animal") | interest Social | Sanity +param per visit | `.param` = 10; `Colonist.lua:2675-2678` |
| Gambler | `CasinoComplexBase` only (not MegaMall) | Sanity −param per visit, 50% chance | `.param` = 20; the 50 is a literal, `CasinoComplex.lua:4-9` |
