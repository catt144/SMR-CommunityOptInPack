### D19 — Arboretum (design, content) `[built - gameplay acceptance owed]`

Owner, 2026-10-03: build "a new service type building people could use like an arboretum,
that would give relaxation / comfort bonuses, something higher end than some of the other
types like parks, that would consume seeds"; "proceed with the Arboretum … a deep dive and
a module build of that to test with." This is an opt-in test build under FIX_POLICY §0,
on placeholder vanilla art. The separate service category and ordinary garden footprint,
one per dome, are provisional starting positions, not owner rulings (OI-47 and OI-48).

**Owner, 2026-10-03: the Arboretum ships after launch, not in the launch set**
(`docs/agent/prompts/Launch_Prep/README.md`, `7f9c0e6`). The module's code and records are
committed; its `metadata.lua` and `items.lua` hunks (the toggle, its default and three code-list
lines) and its `RELEASE_OUTBOX.md` Pending entry are held uncommitted in the working tree until
launch is done, so HEAD's shipping lists do not load it. OI-47 and OI-48 wait until after launch.

**Implementation.** `Code/Opt_Arboretum.lua`, the `SMROptInArboretum` template and generated
class. Author template data in `tools/arboretum/template.json`; regenerate with
`python tools/arboretum/generate.py`. No vanilla class methods are changed.
The base inherits `LifeSupportConsumerService` and `ElectricityConsumer`. Native
`HasConsumption` requests Seeds and charges each visit. The placeholder is the vanilla
Large Garden's `GardenLargeCP3`, with its icon and build shape.

**Starting balance (design choices, not measurements).** Comfort 15, Sanity 10, capacity 40,
10 visitors (matching the donor entity's template), four-hour visits, 0.5 Seeds per visit,
10 Seeds stored. Power 5, water 1, Polymer maintenance 1; construction 20 Concrete and
5 Polymers. Interests: Relaxation, Exercise, Playing. No jobs. The separate category adds
coverage alongside Parks; the native Hippie Parks bonus does not apply to this category.
ServiceInterestTags reads the new building's interests/category through native methods.

**Toggle and availability.** Off by default; live OFF hides new construction but placed
Arboretums continue operating. ON offers the template once Seeds are available.
`NoTerraforming` hides it. Explicit per-module veto also hides it. Existing buildings remain
registered with either toggle state; neither switch is an uninstall test.

**Persisted names.** `Arboretum` is the account option/Register key.
`SMROptInArboretum` is the template, placed class, `template_name`, native label and
dome service-category key. `SMROptInArboretumBase` is its parent class. The permanent keys
are `SMROptInArboretumBase:SMROptInArboretum` and
`LifeSupportConsumerService:SMROptInArboretumBase`. No new GameVar, object field, modifier,
custom entity or saved callback is introduced. Native objects retain their requests,
stored Seeds, visitor/holder references and service assignment tables in a save.

**Removal (SOURCE/INFERRED, not a loaded-save result).** Turning the module off leaves
all that content intact. Removing the mod leaves the saved template/category strings,
object data and class references; the placed class's missing custom fallback resolves to
`UnpersistedMissingClass`, not a functioning vanilla garden. Demolish all Arboretums and
save before removal. Even a demolished-content save can retain class references; no claim
of clean removal. A cold removal load, with and without placed content, remains unmeasured.

**Controls and reach.** `tools/arboretum/deskcheck.py` executes archived native consumption
bodies with synthetic requests: the last debit must stop working, and the drone callback
must restore it. Build locks test OFF/ON, unavailable Seeds, NoTerraforming, a stale active
status plus veto, and a foreign garden. The loaded-game path remains U until the owner
builds, stocks and observes visitors; no seeds-per-sol claim comes from these controls.
