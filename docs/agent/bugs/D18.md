### D18 — Elevator Depot (design, content)  `[built — Code/Opt_ElevatorDepot.lua + ElevatorDepot_10_ElevatorDepot.lua; dev-mod sittings passed live; the shipping battery is brief 35's]`

⚖️ **Owner rulings.** These are in the train spec
`docs/agent/reports/TRAIN_LOGISTICS_DESIGN_20260917.md` §11:
- two manually placed halves, one pair per colony, with the survivor rule;
- surface-owned Import / Export rows, no Balanced, default Import;
- the cabin loads to the destination's need, lowest stock first, on both legs;
- 250 at base and 500 after the depot's own upgrade (10 Metals, 10 Concrete);
- Drone Access default off, no crew of its own;
- native train buttons.

At brief 34's checkpoint (2026-10-02, OI-41) its names stay exactly as they are, `Dev` included.
That covers the class and template `SMROptInElevatorDepotDev`, the base
`SMROptInElevatorDepotDevBase` and the upgrade `SMROptInElevatorDepotDev_Capacity`, which are save
contract and never shown to the player. The 2026-09-29 stand-in `SMROptInElevatorStationDev`
gets no compatibility.

**Code.** `Code/Opt_ElevatorDepot.lua` registers it and holds the build-menu lock. Its part is
`ElevatorDepot_10_ElevatorDepot.lua`. The console table is `SMRElevatorDepot`; the old names
`SMRElevatorDepotDev` and `SMRElevatorStationDev` stay as aliases for the shared TestKit's
installed slots. The template is `Data/BuildingTemplate/SMROptInElevatorDepotDev.lua` with its
generated class, and the entities are `SMROptInElevatorDepot` and `…Receiver`. It needs neither
D16 nor D17.

**Controls.**
- `module_gates_smoke.py`: the lock (off hides the depot, on offers it).
- `tools/trains/depot/tests/` (wiring, props and revision): the depot's behaviour.
- `crossing_witness_smoke.py`: the cabin's cross-map transfer. Brief 33's attended check proved
  the crossings net on the dev mod.

**Owed.** The shipping battery (brief 35; audit §2), with OI-38's scripted up-leg read. Removing
the mod with a placed pair is unsupported (FIX_POLICY §0); the Mod Options help says to demolish
both halves first.
