- ⇒ **Between two mods, whichever the account enabled FIRST loads first**, and its
  wrappers therefore sit INNERMOST when both wrap the same function. The order is
  stable per account and survives restarts (it lives in account storage), but it is
  not something a mod can declare, request or detect ahead of its own load.

- ⚠️ **`Mod.lua:490-521` is the OTHER order and must not be confused with this one:**
  `ModDef:LoadCode` iterates `ipairs(self.code)`, so INTRA-mod file order is the
  `metadata.lua` `code` list's order and scans no directory. Two different orderings,
  two different sources — intra-mod is ours to set, inter-mod is not.

- ⇒ **Design consequence, and it is why this fact was pinned:** a companion mod may
  not assume it loads after (or before) another. Both the Community Fix Pack and the
  Community Opt-In Pack wrap `UniversalRocketBase:GetFuelResourceRequest` and
  `Drone:CleanUnreachables`; each pair was shown order-independent by construction
  (mutually exclusive conditions in the first, pre-wrapper vs post-wrapper in the
  second) rather than by assuming a load order.

- ⚠️ **What is NOT measured here:** the observed order on the owner's rig. The route
  above says it is decided by the enable order in account storage; the rig's actual
  order is read off a boot log, and the `split-optins` verification leg records it.
  Until then, "the opt-in mod was enabled later, so it loads second" is an inference
  from the route, not an observation.
