- **The fastest PLAYER-REACHABLE game speed is 5×, not the 20× that
  `const.ultraGameSpeed` advertises.** `const.ultraGameSpeed = 20` is real
  (`Lua\_GameConst.lua:26-28`) but `"ultra"` is appended to the speed-state list
  **only under `if Platform.debug`** (`Lua\X\HUD.lua:462-467`, `:481-487`), and
  the HUD only wires `"play"` / `"medium"` / `"fast"` buttons
  (`Lua\XDef\HUD.generated.lua:226,:247,:269`). Retail speeds are therefore
  **1× / 3× (`mediumGameSpeed`) / 5× (`fastGameSpeed`)** — confirmed in game by
  the fastest button's own tooltip, *"Play at five times normal speed"*.
  ⚠️ **Any real-time-vs-game-time arithmetic must use 5× as the ceiling.** Cost
  a wrong figure on 2026-08-02: an F82 estimate assumed 20×, computed 3.3 sols
  where the truth is 0.83, and wrongly dismissed the owner's own "close to an
  entire sol" report as reading low. **One sol at each retail speed:** 12 real
  min at 1×, 4 at 3×, **2 min 24 s at 5×** (`const.Scale.sols` = 720 000 ms,
  `Lua\__const.lua:1454-1458`).
