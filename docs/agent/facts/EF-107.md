- **A HABITAT ADOPTS NEIGHBOURING DOMES' INTERIOR WORKPLACES AT BUILD TIME, AND THE LIST NEVER GROWS.**
  `Workforce:GameInit` (`Lua/Buildings/Workforce.lua:10-13`) calls `AddOutskirtBuildings`, and the base
  body (`:138-144`) adds every `DomeOutskirtBld` inside `GetOutsideWorkplacesDist() + 7` hexes that
  passes `IsBuildingInWorkRange`. Every `Workplace` is a `DomeOutskirtBld` (`Workplace.lua:6`).
  `Dome:AddOutskirtBuildings` (`Dome.lua:1127-1137`) carries the guard `if not dome or dome == self`,
  which skips a building inside another dome; **the base body a habitat inherits has no such guard.**
  A workplace built later registers through `DomeOutskirtBld:AddToDomeLabels` (`DomeOutskirtBld.lua:23-43`),
  which adds an in-dome building to its own dome and returns, so the habitat never sees it.
- **The two job tests disagree, and the disagreement is what holds habitat residents at home.**
  The emigration scorer `Colonist:GetBestReachableCommunities` (`Colonist.lua:3425-3494`) asks
  `Workforce:HasFreeWorkplacesAroundForSpecialist` (`Workforce.lua:83-113`), whose per-building test is
  `b.ui_working and b:CanWorkHere(colonist) and b:HasFreeWorkSlots()`. `Workplace:CanWorkHere` (`:809-821`)
  checks specialist enforcement and incompatible traits only. The job picker `ChooseWorkplace` uses
  `IsSuitableWorkplace` (`Workplace.lua:1447-1462`), which refuses any building with a valid
  `parent_dome` for a colonist whose `residence` is a `MicroGHabitatBase`. So a listed in-dome building
  with a free slot is worth `WorkplacesEval` = 10 (`Workforce.lua:116-128`) to the stay score and is
  never a job the resident can take: they read "Unemployed" and stay.
- **What the scorer reads, and what it does not.** `Community:GetScoreFor` (`Community.lua:442-460`) is
  life support (100 / -400), the trait filter, and the best comfort among residences whose
  `filter_residents ~= "Everyone"`. It never reads `stat_comfort`, service quality, or a count of jobs
  or beds: the job term is a flag worth 10, the bed term only matters to a homeless colonist. A dome
  failing `Dome:CanAcceptNewColonists` (`Dome.lua:3446`: `accept_colonists and not supply_interrupted
  and ui_working`) is dropped before it is scored. A tie with work available at home means stay
  (`better_eval` needs `new_eval > eval`; `better_work` needs `not work_available`).
