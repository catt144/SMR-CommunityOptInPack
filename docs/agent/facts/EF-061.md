- **The threshold is a quorum over parameters, not a single bar.**
  `UpdateTerraformingThreshold` (`Lua/Terraforming.lua:41-61`) walks every
  `TerraformingParam` preset, counts how many declare a `ThresholdItem` with
  the id being tested AND are still below it, and only messages
  `TerraformThresholdPassed(id, true)` when that count reaches zero.
  `AtmosphereBreathable` is declared by **two** params — **Atmosphere ≥ 95%**
  (`Data/TerraformingParam.lua:86`) and **Temperature ≥ 50%** (`:143`), each
  with `Hysteresis` 2. Water and Vegetation carry no such item, so they do not
  gate breathability at all. The same quorum shape governs every other shared
  threshold id (`ToxicRainStop`, `AtmosphereCleared`, …) — do not read a
  single param's number as the whole condition.
- **What breathability alone switches, with no law involved**
  (`SetAtmosphereBreathable`, `Lua/Terraforming.lua:304-333`): a
  `disable_air_consumption` +1 modifier on label `Building`; an
  `electricity_consumption` −`const.Terraforming.OpenDome_ElectricityReduction`
  (= 50) modifier on label `Dome`; `StatusEffect_Suffocating` /
  `_Suffocating_Outside` / `_Freezing` cleared on every colonist; and the
  dome-walk gate flips from `dome_walk_dist` to `dome_passage_dist`
  (`Lua/Units/Colonist.lua:1560`). Domes stop needing oxygen HERE, not at the
  law.
- **What the law adds on top.** `SetOpenAirBuildings(true)`
  (`Lua/Buildings/OpenAirBuilding.lua:107-165`) swaps every `OpenAirBuilding`
  to its `_Open` entity through `OpenAirChangeThread`, applies
  +`OpenDome_InsideBuildingsMaintenanceIncrease` (= 10%) to label
  `InsideBuildings`, and arms the −`OpenDome_CostReduction` (= 50%) discount
  that `OnMsg.ModifyConstructionCost` (`:99-105`) applies to
  `OpenAirBuilding`-derived templates. Const values `Lua/__const.lua:1836-1849`.
- **`SetOpenAirBuildings(true)` has exactly two callers tree-wide** — the
  `Policy_OpenDomes` law's `OnStart` (`Data/LawDef/LawDef-Research.lua:646-655`,
  itself re-checking `BreathableAtmosphere`) and `choice1_func` of the
  `AtmosphereBreathableNoPoliticsRule` popup
  (`Data/PopupNotifications/PopupNotificationPreset-GreenMars.lua:15-18`).
  Under the `NoPolitics` game rule the law does not exist (its `Prerequisite`
  excludes `NoTerraforming` and `NoPolitics`) and that popup's button is the
  only route.
- ⚠️ **Backsliding overrides `can_be_repealed = false`.** Falling under the
  threshold (hysteresis 2 ⇒ 93% / 48%) runs `SetAtmosphereBreathable(false)`,
  which force-closes the domes and calls `ActiveLaws.Policy_OpenDomes:
  Deactivate()` (`Lua/Terraforming.lua:348-355`) — the player cannot repeal
  this law, but the engine can.
- ⚖️ **What is measured and what is not.** Every citation above was READ off
  the shipped tree on 2026-08-16; the state machine was not instrumented. The
  first live observation the same day: the *Planet Habitable* popup fired and
  the law was not reachable in the politics grid — see `EF-062` for why that is
  a second, unrelated gate.
- ⭐ **THE QUORUM CLAIM IS CONFIRMED LIVE (2026-08-16, owner's campaign).**
  Their terraforming panel at breathability read **Atmosphere 100% ·
  Temperature 84.09% · Water 100% · Vegetation 40%** — breathable reached with
  **Vegetation at 40%**, which is the falsifiable half of this fact: if
  Vegetation declared an `AtmosphereBreathable` threshold anywhere at or above
  40 the state could not have been reached. It does not, and it was. The
  Atmosphere/Temperature bars are confirmed as *satisfied*, not as *exact*
  (both readings sit well past their thresholds, so the 95/50 numbers
  themselves remain source-only).
