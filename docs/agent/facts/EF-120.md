- **CANDIDATE ? observed matcher model, not the C implementation.** For an otherwise
  eligible storage-to-storage request pair S ? D, the observed decisions fit
  **want OR rank OR push**: D is below its Desired Amount; D's Desired is above S's;
  or S holds more than its own Desired. The source's Desired is not a withdrawal floor.
  This is an observational characterization, not a complete necessary-and-sufficient
  truth table or a promise that every eligible pair will be chosen.

  witnessed storage-to-storage choice with none of want/rank/push falsifies this model.
  A controlled source below its Desired with an assignable requesting destination is
  the floor control: native pairing contradicts a source-floor rule. To promote an exact
  truth table, vary one branch at a time with the others false, read both supply Desired
  values directly, include eligible nonchosen alternatives and cover equality/booking
  edges. No new owner sitting is requested for this candidate.
