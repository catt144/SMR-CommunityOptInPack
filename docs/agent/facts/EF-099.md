### SMRTK 03A correction — placement is not a fit test (2026-09-13)

- **SOURCE, no construction dry-run flag.** The complete signature at
  `ConstructionSite.lua:2193` is `(city, class_name, pos, angle, params,
  no_block_pass, no_flatten)`. It creates a site at :2203, flattens at :2222
  and applies grids at :2224. The final two booleans suppress particular
  side effects; neither makes placement a test. A building plan must avoid
  calling this function entirely. The grid line placers' `test` parameter
  does not establish a construction-site test parameter.
- **SOURCE, terrain filter only.** `IsBuildableZoneQR` at
  `BuildableGrid.lua:310-312` delegates to `CurrentMap.buildable:IsBuildable`.
  It does not enumerate existing occupants or validate a rotated building's
  entire footprint. The earlier stamper inference of a complete per-hex fit
  check was too broad; callers need independent footprint/obstruction checks.
- **SOURCE, completion is colony-wide on the current map.**
  `Cheats.lua:89-99` visits every current-map construction site twice and
  finishes wires/pipes first. It is not bounded to a replay's newly created
  objects. A bounded stamper must complete its own sites or refuse unrelated
  live construction before using that sweep.
