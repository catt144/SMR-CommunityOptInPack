  **`GetGridGlobalStorage`**, **`PlanetaryAsteroidVisitPossible`**,

  **`const.MinDaysFoodSupplyBeforeNotification`**,
  **`const.UnreachablesCleanupDeltaT`**.

  missing three are still present (`ColonistMaxDomeWalkDist`,
  `ColonistMinDistToIgnorePassage`), the three appear **nowhere in the tree**,

  `_GameConst.lua` yet present in 20 files. ⚠️ Residual: a const living only

  useful part. `Fix_LastTransmissionStorage` (F75) has no `Require` gate and
  installs a closure calling `GetGridGlobalStorage` at *evaluation* time,

  **`ScriptCheckGridGlobalStorage`** (`Fix_LastTransmissionStorage.lua:82-90`)
