- ⚠️ **A DELIVERY IN FLIGHT IS RE-OPENED BY `Drone:ImproveDemandRequest`
  TOWARD ANY STRICTLY-HIGHER-BAND DEMAND OF THE SAME RESOURCE.** (Source-read
  2026-09-01, the bands report's review — the gap the report's V/R11 row
  missed.) `Drone:Deliver` (`Lua/Units/Drone.lua:1164-1175`) calls
  `self:ImproveDemandRequest(…)` for every delivery that came from `PickUp`
  unless `do_not_improve_req` is passed — and the shipped `PickUp` tail chains
  `SetCommand("Deliver", d_request)` WITHOUT it (`:1013-1014`).
  `ImproveDemandRequest` (`:760-812`) computes `priority =
  d_building:GetPriorityForRequest(d_request) + 1` (`:766`) and asks
  `FindDemandRequest(self, resource, amount, priority, ignore_flags)` for a
  strictly better destination in the REAL demand queues, unassigning the
  original on success (`:790-796`). **Consequence for urgency designs:** a
  demand elevated only OUTSIDE the real queues (a view tier, a substituted
  pairing) still carries its real band in `GetPriorityForRequest`, so the
  parts flown for a tier-5 repair can be traded up mid-flight to any routine
  band-3 demand — the exact inversion the tiers exist to prevent. Guard the
  seam: a chained wrapper on `ImproveDemandRequest` (synchronous — finders +
  claim arithmetic, no yield in the body) that declines improvement while
  `d_request` is tier-tracked, or pass the vanilla `do_not_improve_req` seam.
  Vanilla baseline for scale: band-2 deliveries are ALREADY hijackable by
  band-3 demands today — the trap is only that tier designs think they
  outrank 3 and this seam does not know it. E-8's card must assert the guard
  (a tier delivery arrives at the tier building). Cross-refs: `EF-060` (the
  finders), opt-in `reports/DRONE_BANDS_CLEAN_REVERT_20260901.md` §4.2/§5.
