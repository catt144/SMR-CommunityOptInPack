- **How it was measured.** A log-only wrapper on `TaskRequestHub:FindTask`
  (route: `EF-060`) witnessed every pairing the C matcher returned during a
  10-game-hour window on a staged copy of the owner's `C47FARM` — 25,184
  calls, 1,304 supply+demand pairings, with a per-pairing counterfactual: the
  nearest depot whose supply request read `GetActualAmount() > 0` and
  `CanAssignUnit()` at that instant, with its distance from the drone.
- **The elimination chain, four experiments:** distance — the devs' own
  scattered-source brake (`supply_dist_modifier = 150`) applied to all 3,390
  live vegetation requests, provably rebaked, routing unchanged
  (`archive/c48brake_*`); demand-side gating — the owner built a diner in the
  worst-traffic cluster and five drones bulk-filled it to exactly 100%
  including a final sub-load piece, so consumption demand accepts partial
  loads (its `rfWaitToFill` is the ≥1-resource-unit guard the vanilla
  comments describe, `DroneControl.lua:506`, `ResourceStockpile.lua:689-690`);
  bucket order — hub queue census put veg at p1 and depots mostly at p0 with
  a few at p2/p3, and the higher-bucket depots also never won; ⇒ the one
  discriminator left is the `rfStorageDepot` brand itself.
- **The corollaries** (each read live, same logs): every one of 34 Seeds
  depots offered its FULL stock all window — e.g. `StorageSeeds#14612` stored
  180,000, request actual 180,000, `desired_amount` 65,000, assignable — so
  `desired_amount` does not shrink the supply side; 125 of 479 Seeds pairings
  had a DEPOT as the destination (the hoard grows from the landscape); 134 of
  506 Food pairings chose a `VegetationTaskRequester` (grown crops offer Food
  — the owner's "picking food off the ground"); `Diner#8326` drained 20,000
  in 10 game hours and never stalled.
- ⚖️ **What is and is not claimed:** the C matcher's internal scan order is
  unread — this is a black-box characterization from 985 decisions with
  counterfactuals, on one colony. The behavioral rule is categorical in that
  sample; the mechanism NAME (`rfStorageDepot` special-casing) is the only
  hypothesis left standing, not a source citation.
