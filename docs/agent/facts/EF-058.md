- ⭐ **The chain, and the trap inside it.** `Legislature:RecalcPoliciesState`
  (`Lua/Factions/Legislature.lua:1085-1145`) rebuilds, per `PolicyCategoryDef`,
  a state for every `PolicyDef` in it: `hidden`, `locked` or `visible`. It
  iterates in `SortKey` order and accumulates as it goes, so each slot is
  judged against the ones before it. A slot is `visible` iff it is the first
  non-hidden slot in the row, or `are_prev_prepared` (`:1075-1084`) holds. That
  helper returns false for any earlier slot that is `locked` **or is `visible`
  but not yet prepared** — so leaving a revealed policy unprepared freezes the
  entire remainder of its category. This is the non-obvious part: revealing a
  slot is not progress, PREPARING it is.
- **Caps and bypasses.** `starting_amount = 3` policies per category are
  candidates; building a **Martian Assembly** raises the cap to 10,000,000,
  i.e. removes it — and the `Governance` category is not rendered at all
  without one (`:1087-1098`). The `UnlockedPolicies` game rule turns every
  `locked` into `visible` (`:1122-1131`), as does `g_AllPoliciesVisibleCheat`.
  (Note `martian_assembly_amount = has_MartianAssembly and 7 or 0` at `:1089`
  is computed and never read — dead, harmless, do not "fix" it.)
- **`hidden` is a different thing and does NOT block the chain.** A policy is
  hidden when no law of it passes its `Prerequisite` (`has_visible_laws`,
  `:1056-1064`); `LawDef.Prerequisite` is documented *"if not met, the law will
  not be shown in the UI"*
  (`Lua/ClassDefs/ClassDef-Factions.generated.lua:1748`). `are_prev_prepared`
  skips hidden slots, so a DLC- or rule-gated policy cannot deadlock a row.
- **A preparation costs a whole session.** `SetSessionAction("prepare", …)`
  ends the session and cycles it (`:783-787`). Sessions are
  `const.Factions.LegislatureBetweenSessions` = 1440000ms = **1 Sol**
  (`Lua/__const.lua:754-759`; `const.Scale.sols = 24 × const.Scale.h`, so the
  scaled value is exactly one day). Two accelerators exist: the
  `EfficientAssembly` law grants one *instant* preparation per session that
  does not spend the session action (`:767-781`), and a **Law Office**
  (Assembly of Planets sponsor, `DLC/thomas/Code/LawOffice.lua:41-63`)
  auto-prepares a RANDOM `visible`-and-unprepared policy about every 3 sols —
  untargeted, but it does grind a row down unattended.
- ⛔ **Faction suggestions cannot surface a locked policy.**
  `Legislature:ChooseSessionAction` (`:297-340`) only considers a law when
  `policy_visibility_data.state == "visible"`, so "Open Floor" will never
  propose anything still behind a `?`, no matter how many sessions cycle.
- ⛔ **A locked hex never names itself.** Its rollover is the generic
  `"Law not available"` / `"Prepare more laws in the category to unlock this
  law"` (`Data/XDef/LawEntry.lua:155`, `:212`); only non-locked states list the
  policy's laws (`:186-203`). So a policy can be *announced* elsewhere and be
  undiscoverable by inspecting the grid.
- ⚠️ **The worked example that produced this fact.** `Policy_OpenDomes` is
  `SortKey 900`, 10th of 11 in `ResearchAndSafety` (Research & Ecology). Its
  own gate — breathable atmosphere, `EF-057` — can clear and fire a popup
  saying *"The Open Domes law has been unlocked"* while the slot is still an
  unnamed `?` eight preparations away. Nothing connects the two systems:
  `SetAtmosphereBreathable` never calls into the Legislature, so there is no
  broken reveal path — the omission is by construction. Parked QoL options:
  this repo's `docs/FUTURE_IDEAS.md` §8.
- ⭐⭐ **CONFIRMED BY A PLAYER, SAME DAY (2026-08-16, owner's own campaign).**
  The owner reached `Policy_OpenDomes` the organic way — no cheat, terraforming
  taken to breathable and the law actually passed — and reports they **had to
  work their way down the row**, preparing each earlier `ResearchAndSafety`
  slot in turn. That is exactly what `are_prev_prepared` predicts, and it is
  the prediction that could most easily have been wrong: had the slot appeared
  on its own, this fact would have been rewritten instead of confirmed. The
  chain is therefore what a real run costs, not an artefact of reading the
  code.
- ⚖️ **What is measured and what is not.** Read off the shipped tree
  2026-08-16; the state machine was never instrumented in a run. The player
  confirmation above covers the CHAIN only — the 3-per-category cap, the
  Martian Assembly lift, the `UnlockedPolicies` bypass, the Law Office
  auto-prepare and the 1-Sol session cadence were never isolated or exercised
  and stay source-only. The earlier evidence — a screenshot of the owner's grid
  (Research & Ecology showing two revealed slots and the rest `?`, Governance
  rendered ⇒ Martian Assembly present) plus two cycled sessions that offered no
  dome option — was only *consistent* with the code; the player confirmation
  above is what actually carries the chain.
