- **ANY ENABLED MOD BLOCKS ALL ACHIEVEMENTS ON EXACTLY `Platform.playstation`, `Platform.xbox` AND
  `Platform.windows_store`, AND THIS PACK CAN NEITHER CAUSE NOR AVOID IT.** `DoModsBlockAchievements()`
  (`CommonLua/Classes/Achievement.lua:61-63`) returns true on those three platforms only. It is consumed
  at `:77` (`ModsLoaded` non-empty → `"mods loaded"`) and in `Lua/UI/SaveLoad.lua:102`. Steam is not in
  the list; the Microsoft Store (Game Pass) is a PC platform and IS in it, which is why player-facing
  text says "Steam and other PC versions" and never "PC" (FIX_POLICY §7).
- **The pack touches none of the blockers** that `EngineCanUnlockAchievement` checks (demo, tutorial,
  already unlocked, DLC absent, mods loaded on the three platforms) and cannot subscribe to the
  reasons message: `UnableToUnlockAchievementReasons` is on `ModMsgBlacklist`
  (`CommonLua/Modding/Mod.lua:1452`; it was `CommonLua/Classes/Mod.lua:1439` on 1.0.7). The savegame's
  `blocking_achievements` flag is set only from a game rule with `DisableAchievements`
  (`CommonLua/UI/SaveLoadUI.lua:442`), never by a mod.
- **Control.** `grep -n "DoModsBlockAchievements" -A2 <Src>/CommonLua/Classes/Achievement.lua` prints
  the three-platform return; a fourth platform name there, or a Steam name, falsifies the disclosure.
  `grep -rn "UnableToUnlockAchievementReasons" <Src>/CommonLua/Modding/Mod.lua` proves the blacklist
  entry; its absence would mean a mod could at least observe the block. Re-read both on every game
  update.
- **Why it is a fact and not policy.** FIX_POLICY §7 carried this mechanism as prose until 2026-09-16
  (the wording had said "not on Steam/PC" until 2026-08-16, which would have misled a Game Pass
  player). The rule that survives in §7 is the wording rule; the mechanism lives here. Related:
  EF-094 (how achievement state is read, cleared and tested).
