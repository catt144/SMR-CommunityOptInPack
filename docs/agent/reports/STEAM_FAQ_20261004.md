```
[h1]FAQ — Train Hub, Elevator Depot and the station sliders[/h1]
Answers for the three train modules in [i]Relaunched Fix Pack: Opt-In Modules[/i]. Built and tested on game version 1.1.1.

[h2]Getting started[/h2]
[b]How do I turn these on?[/b]
Options → Mod Options. Station import/export rows, Train Hub and Elevator Depot each have their own switch, and all three are off until you turn them on. A switch takes effect as soon as you press Apply, in a running game too.

[b]Do I need the Relaunched Fix Pack?[/b]
No. The modules work with it or without it.

[b]Where are the new buildings?[/b]
In the Stations category of the build menu, once their switch is on. The Elevator Depot also needs the underground unlocked. Both are built outside domes, and neither can be built on an asteroid.

[b]Do I need all three?[/b]
No. The Elevator Depot needs neither of the others. The station rows work with or without a hub. The only link is that the rows are always on while the Train Hub is on.

[h2]The sliders (station import/export rows)[/h2]
[b]What does this add?[/b]
Every resource row on a Train Station gets four settings and a slider. Click the row to step through the settings.
[list]
[*][b]Balanced[/b] (the game's default): trains exchange stock with other stations to hold the amount you picked. Surplus in nearby storage stays where it is.
[*][b]Export[/b]: trains take away everything above the amount you picked and never deliver here.
[*][b]Import[/b]: trains bring stock in up to the amount you picked and never take it away.
[*][b]Not accepted[/b]: the station stops storing that resource. Trains and drones may carry off what is left.
[/list]

[b]What does the slider set?[/b]
The target for that resource, as a share of the station's capacity for it. Hover the row and the tooltip shows the share and the amount it comes to.

[b]Will Export drain my depots?[/b]
No. For an Export row, drones only bring in what nearby storage holds above its own Desired Amount. An Import row is the opposite case: local drones may drain that station to zero.

[b]The tooltip says "No drones in range — trains only". What does that mean?[/b]
No Drone Hub covers that station, so only trains move its stock.

[b]Do I need a Train Hub for this?[/b]
No. Without a hub each station keeps its own settings. A station that joins a hub's lines uses the hub's separate settings instead, and gets its own back if it is disconnected.

[b]What happens if I turn the rows off?[/b]
Stations go back to the game's own import and export requests. Your settings stay stored on the station and come back if you turn the rows on again. While the Train Hub switch is on, the rows stay on.

[h2]Train Hub[/h2]
[b]What is it?[/b]
A junction where three straight train lines cross, so cargo can change lines. It stores resources for the stations on its lines, runs its own drones to build and repair track, and has four upgrades.

[b]What does it cost?[/b]
60 Concrete, 40 Metals, 15 Electronics and 10 Machine Parts. It is maintained with Electronics.

[b]How do the rows work on a hub's lines?[/b]
The same four settings, with the hub in the middle: Export sends stock above your minimum to the hub, and Import brings stock from the hub up to your amount. A full hub refuses exports.

[b]How do I add trains at a hub?[/b]
You don't. The hub never sends out a train of its own. Use Send out Train at an ordinary Train Station on the line.

[b]What are the upgrades?[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Capacity Network Upgrade[/b] (20 Metals, 20 Concrete): +100% Station storage, +100% Train cargo and passenger capacity.
[*][b]Train Cargo Upgrade[/b] (40 Metals, 20 Polymers): +100% Train cargo capacity, +25% Train speed.
[*][b]Power Upgrade[/b] (30 Metals, 20 Electronics): +75 Power production per hub, heats the ground in drone range, and trains lose the cold penalty.
[*][b]Storage Hub[/b] (60 Metals, 30 Machine Parts): +100% storage in every Train Hub, +19 Power consumption per hub.
[/list]
Each one is a colony upgrade: buy it once and any hub can switch it.

[b]Why did my stations stop spoiling food?[/b]
While the Train Hub switch is on, Train Stations don't spoil food. The hub itself still does. Switch the module off and stations spoil food again, as in the unmodded game.

[b]What happens if I turn the Train Hub off?[/b]
No new hubs can be built. Hubs already built keep working, with their drones, their upgrades and the rows on their lines.

[h2]Elevator Depot[/h2]
[b]What is it?[/b]
A cargo elevator for trains. It has two halves, one on the surface and one underground, joined by a cabin that carries cargo between them. Build one half on each side and connect each to its train line. Each half costs 5 Concrete, 2 Metals and 1 Machine Part.

[b]How do I choose what it carries?[/b]
On the surface half, set each resource to Import (goes down), Export (comes up) or Not accepted (the cabin never carries it). Every resource starts on Import. The underground half shows the same settings but can't change them. The slider on a surface row sets how much the surface half gathers on Import, or keeps on Export.

[b]How much does it carry, and how often?[/b]
The cabin makes one trip per game hour, down and then up, over and over. It carries up to 250 per trip and loads what the other side needs, lowest stock first. The Expanded Depot upgrade (10 Metals, 10 Concrete) raises that to 500 per trip and storage to 500 per resource at both ends. You buy it once for the pair, from either half.

[b]Do drones use it?[/b]
Only if you allow it. Drone Access is off on both halves to begin with, and each half has its own button. With it off, trains and the cabin still move the cargo. The depot has no drones of its own.

[b]Can I build more than one?[/b]
One pair per colony. Once a half stands on a map, the build menu greys it out there.

[b]What if only one half is built?[/b]
The cabin stays idle and that half works as a plain Train Station. Its settings are kept for when the other half exists.

[b]Does it change the game's own elevator?[/b]
No. The game's elevator is untouched.

[b]What happens if I turn the Elevator Depot off?[/b]
No new depot can be built. A pair already built keeps working.

[h2]Removing the mod[/h2]
[b]Can I just uninstall it?[/b]
Not with hubs or depots standing. They exist only while the mod is installed, so do this first:
[olist]
[*]Demolish every Train Hub and both halves of every Elevator Depot, then save the game.
[*]If you used the Drone dials, set both back to base, press Apply and save.
[*]Then disable or remove the mod and restart the game fully.
[/olist]
Removing the mod while hubs or depots are still standing leaves buildings in your save that the game no longer knows, and the game reports errors when that save loads. With them demolished first, your stations go back to the game's own import and export requests.

[b]Is removal completely clean after that?[/b]
Not guaranteed. References to the custom building classes can remain in the save after demolition and may produce missing-class warnings when loaded without the mod. Bought Train Hub upgrades also remain recorded in your save, and their ordinary game bonuses may remain after the mod is removed.

[h2]Bugs and questions[/h2]
Each module is written up on the mods' site. Bugs can be reported there from a browser, with no account needed, and a save or a log can be attached privately. Replies in this thread work too.
[url=https://catt144.github.io/SMR-CommunityMods/]https://catt144.github.io/SMR-CommunityMods/[/url]
```
