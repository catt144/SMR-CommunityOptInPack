## Bugs the sitting found, fixed in-link

- The crest handoff was the file default, although L3 had accepted `HandoffAt="outside"` (`1b32bc8`).
- The hub dispatched before a new site's resource requests existed, so the claim held nothing
  (`68ad91b`).
- The runtime XTemplate panel section never rendered in play; every mocked smoke passed it
  (`c73a286`).
- `door_count` crashed the flight driver: the engine's `GetSpotBeginIndex` raises "Invalid spot" on a
  name the body lacks and never returns -1 (log `Mars.exe-20260925-13.34.56`, 36 errors). The mock
  returned -1 (`ad5113b`, which counts by `CObject.HasSpot` and makes the mock raise).

## Explained, not changed

- The "Construction not serviced by drones" rows were stale while the game was paused; vanilla
  refreshes them through game-time `Notify`, and they cleared on unpause.
- The dome outside the 15-hex ring is genuinely uncovered. "Not enough Power" was unbuilt power lines.
- A meteor beside the hub destroyed it before `5cfaee6`, and vanilla stored a train bound for it.
- The ruin's magenta icon was brief 06's uncommitted `display_icon` test path, routed to 06's
  session.
- Supplies vanish under a quick-built hub: vanilla's cheat path deletes the piles under the footprint
  (`ConstructionSite:OnQuickBuild` → `DestroyStockpilesUnderneath`, `ConstructionSite.lua:1682-1683`,
  `:1637-1643`), where a normal build moves them out (`:129`).
