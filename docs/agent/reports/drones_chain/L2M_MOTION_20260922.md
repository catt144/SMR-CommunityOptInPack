## Time, AI and tunables

SOURCE: segment time arithmetic, `arrival`, `work_done` and `removed` are unchanged for a normal
trip. Motion compilation does not write those offsets. Samples use elapsed absolute GameTime;
pause resubmits nothing. Rendering predicts at most 100 game ms, bounded by a semantic segment
end. An early visual holds at the boundary; a late sample catches up to the absolute position,
changes to the deadline's phase, or removes an expired visual. It never shifts a job deadline.
A long frame can therefore cause a visible correction. There is no variable native-solver ETA.

L4 still owns persisted deadlines and economic completion, and must derive the visual's start
from that authority, including after load or changed route/time constants. `work_done` still
includes constructEnd. A manual early recall establishes its own removal time, with braking;
an already inbound normal trip preserves its existing removal deadline. Plans, curves, heading
and recall state are ephemeral records, with no new saved fields or game-time command frames.
The save gate and console cancellation remain unchanged.

The smoke makes these methods fail if called on its motion fixture, verifies battery top-up,
and checks removal on a command/controller change. The guard now also rejects `run_cmd_on_land`

**Retired constants: none.** The old axis sampler and one-shot corner SetAngle are retired.
`Speed` and `ClimbRate` now budget nominal speed, not a hard instantaneous cap: fixed-deadline
